# Research codebook

**Design for approval · 12 November 2026**

The live codebook is a proposed exception to RTI’s capture-only contract. The old Dossiers pass sent new turns and known names, then kept the longer description (parent of `6fbf1814`). Retain its watermark and bilingual names. Build summaries from cited entries.

**Evidence.** A card has a stable ID, bilingual canonical name when known, aliases, type (brand, person, org, concept or hypothesis), origin (seeded or emerged), pin and entries. Each entry stores a point, valence (positive, negative, mixed or neutral), session/group, turn ID, offset, original words, leg, speaker label/confidence and optional guide question and marker. The model proposes a card, point, valence and turn ID. RTI copies the complete original turn, timestamp and speaker from the batch. Reject unknown turns and unsupported attributions. Upgrades may add a playback anchor and its confidence while preserving the cited live words. [Existing evidence validation](../RTI/Core/Guide/GuideExtraction.swift)

**During the call.** Prepare accepts typed seeds, the selected project’s `codebook-seed.md` and explicit guide hypotheses. The guide parser needs an optional hypotheses field. Register one Research task on the existing scheduler. Send only new settled turns and known card names/aliases. Allow eight new cards per pass. Bind one in-flight pass to the session and project. Commit entries, consumed turn IDs and watermark together. Retry uncommitted turns, including late arrivals. Finish drains the tail and flags incomplete coverage on failure. [Scheduler](../RTI/Sources/Auto/AutoAssistScheduler.swift) · [Guide schema](../RTI/Core/Guide/GuideDocument.swift)

**Files and edits.** Session `codebook.json` owns its evidence. Project `codebook.json` owns card identities, aliases and user edits, and holds merged entries. Serialize project updates across processes, reread under lock, deduplicate by stable entry ID and atomically replace. Session and project `codebook.md` render from their respective JSON. A failed project merge replays from the saved session. Document the versioned schema and link both sets of files from the raw `-rti.md` frontmatter. Freeze the selected business project directory at start. Client notes, personal memory and Git clones are ineligible. Without an eligible project, keep the session copy. Readers accept legacy sessions. [Project selector](../RTI/Core/Session/VaultWorkstreamStore.swift) · [Archive](../RTI/Sources/Session/SessionArchive.swift)

**In the tab.** Sort pinned cards first, then by entry count. Show an entry-derived summary, entries grouped by group, a group filter, valence totals and distinct confidently identified speakers per group. Tap a quote to jump to its session and time. Reuse an unambiguous normalized alias. Offer ambiguous bilingual matches for manual merge. User merges redirect IDs, renames retain aliases and dropped cards stay suppressed on replay. “Different from last group” compares each card’s evidence from this session and the preceding project session, with citations and explicit gaps. A single key in the transcript stars the selected or latest settled turn into session `quotes.jsonl` with its guide question and marker. Respondent cards show seat, roster segment, evidenced brand mentions and top quotes. A spoken name stays inferred until confirmed and contributes no distinct-speaker count.

**Acceptance.** Two invented EN/ZH groups cover alias merge, replay, concurrent finishes, user edits during a pass, exact quote/locator lookup, uncertain seats, and archives predating the codebook. Check exported links offline.
