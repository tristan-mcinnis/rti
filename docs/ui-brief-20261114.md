# RTI: brief for UI mockups (2026-11-14)

Paste this into an image model. Ask for one static Mac window per prompt, 1440 x 900, light mode.

## What RTI is

RTI (Real Time Intelligence) is a native Mac app for one person: a qualitative researcher who runs or watches consumer focus groups and interviews, often in Mandarin, while they happen.

It does three things, in this order of importance:

1. **Capture.** It records the call and shows a live transcript of both sides.
2. **Keep the researcher oriented.** It knows the discussion guide (the list of questions for the session). It shows which question the room is on now, which questions were missed, and what the respondents said under each one.
3. **Help in the moment.** When asked, it gives a short English catch-up of the last few minutes, or one good follow-up question to pass to the moderator, in Mandarin with pinyin and an English gloss.

It does not analyse or write the report. That happens after the call, in other tools.

## Who uses it, and how

- The researcher sits in the back room or on a video call for 60 to 120 minutes.
- They often join late or step out, and need to catch up in 5 seconds.
- They glance at the app between taking notes. Every glance must answer: where are we in the guide, what did I miss, is there anything to ask now.
- They read English fastest. The room speaks Mandarin.

## What the design must feel like

- Calm and quiet, like the Claude or Codex desktop apps: a left sidebar, a large reading area, plenty of white space, near-black text in a regular (not bold) weight, few greys.
- One clear focus per screen. No dashboards of chips and coloured dots.
- Actions (copy, pin, dismiss) sit in a small row directly under the item they act on, not in a far corner.
- Nothing appears without a reason the user can read on screen.

## Screens to imagine

1. **Live, during a group.** Left sidebar: Live, Sessions, Chats, Settings, and the current session's guide sections. Main area: the live transcript. A narrow right panel: "Now asking" (one guide question), "Missed" (at most two questions), and one suggested follow-up question in Mandarin, pinyin and English.
2. **Catch me up.** The same window after the user presses one key: two or three English bullets about the last five minutes. No quotes or pinyin unless asked.
3. **Joining late.** The moment after Record: a small sheet asking "Where is the group now?" with the guide's questions listed, so the user taps one and everything earlier is marked "before recording", not "missed".
4. **What the room said.** A reading view of the session's main ideas, each one the respondents' own Chinese phrase, an English gloss and how often it came up. Opening one shows its quotes in place.

## Words to avoid in the image

Codebook, topic map, extraction, evidence, cell. Use: guide, question, what they said, ideas, quotes.
