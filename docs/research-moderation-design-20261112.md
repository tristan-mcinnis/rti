# Live moderation actions for Research calls

**Proposed for the beta, pending approval.** Use the existing ✦ menu, palette and shortcuts. Each action snapshots the last 10 minutes of final, original speech when invoked. Set minutes independently in Settings → Prompts.

| Action | What the moderator sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Unprobed tension** · ⌘⌥T | Up to three questions the moderator can ask aloud. Each cites the vague claim, euphemism or hedged agreement that prompted it. Ask for a concrete occasion, choice or example. |
| **Said vs done** · new, menu and palette | Pair a recent stated value with that participant’s earlier account of a purchase, action or workaround. Allow the earlier citation to predate the window within this recording. Cite both turns, require a verified identity link and describe behavior as self-reported. |
| **Echo check** · ⌘⌥U | Cite the first opinion and a later turn that echoes or softens it. With a confirmed roster and attributable topic turns, identify seats with no captured turn on this topic. When labels are off, missing or uncertain, show exactly “speaker labels too uncertain for this check”, followed by a topic-level probe with anonymous leg citations. Omit names, seat counts and claims about who stayed silent. |
| **Emerging themes** · ⌘⌥E | Tag each item “raised by a participant” or “raised after a moderator question”. Cite the initiating turn and participant evidence. Discard assent that only repeats a leading question. Distinguish one speaker’s observation from a pattern across verified speakers. Omit items whose origin cannot be established. |

**Evidence on screen.** Show at most five bullets total. Format each citation as `[12:34, Them/S2] "原话"`. The model proposes turn IDs and observations or probes. RTI resolves timestamps, labels and exact original wording locally. Allow only contiguous excerpts of one turn. Validate each bullet before display or storage, including during streaming. If none qualifies, say so. In Guide mode attach the question ID and title at that moment from the marker or Guide assignment. Show “Question unassigned” when unknown.

**Roles and confidence.** Use Prompt 1’s speaker-attribution flag. Word recognition confidence and a configured headcount cannot verify identity. Mic speech supplies moderator framing or questions. In listening-only calls the moderator can be on the system leg; honor an assigned moderator seat there. Preserve unattributed questions as context and exclude them from participant claims. A muted mic supplies no evidence of anyone’s silence.

**Implementation and proof.** Keep `keyTensions`, `probe` and `themes` in `RTI/Core/LLM/AssistantAction.swift`; add `saidVsDone`. Extend the Research menu in `Core/Calls/CallType.swift`. Reuse `Sources/LLM/LLMController+Actions.swift` and the existing chat send path. Defaults live in `Core/LLM/PromptID.swift`; `PromptStore` keeps edited prompts and their drift notices. Preserve remaps and saved action IDs. Supply timed turns since the current transcript formatter omits timestamps. Run all four actions through dispatch and reply validation on invented EN/ZH fixtures covering leading questions, opposing views, an earlier purchase, uncertain labels and listening-only moderation. Assert exact source substrings, times, roles, Guide question, bullet caps and rejection of invented or spliced quotes.
